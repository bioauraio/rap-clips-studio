# lolq school

Sixteen lessons. They are in the order you actually need them: the first one gets a
clip out of the studio today, the rest explain why it came out the way it did and
how to make the next one better.

Every lesson names real buttons. If a lesson says **Generate storyboard**, that is
the exact label on the screen.

From lesson eight onward each one carries an **artefact**: a pack of shot devices
that applies to your project in one click. A lesson with nothing to press at the end
is an article, not a lesson.

## Start here

**[Your first clip, start to finish](00-first-clip.md)** · 8 min · free plan
From an audio file to a downloadable mp4. Includes the honest arithmetic of the
150 free credits and the three ways people burn them by accident.

## The craft

**[Styles: picking one, and blending three](01-styles.md)** · 5 min
Fifteen presets, why the first one you tick matters more than the other two, and
which blends fall apart.

**[Characters: model sheets, attributes, a hero who comes back](02-characters.md)** · 5 min
How a face survives thirty scenes, why attributes live separately from the
character, and why a recurring hero beats a better-looking one-off.

**[Storyboard: editing scenes, redrawing frames](03-storyboard.md)** · 6 min
What to fix on the scene card, when one frame is enough and when the whole scene
has to go. Also: which redraws are free and which are not.

**[Video engines: Grok, Seedance, Kling](04-engines.md)** · 6 min
What you are paying for, engine by engine, in credits and in what you see. Where
the cheap engine is genuinely the right answer.

**[Assembly: grain, 9:16, the finished file](05-assembly.md)** · 5 min
How approved scenes become one file, what film grain actually does, and why a
clip sometimes comes out shorter than the track.

## When it goes wrong

**[It came out bad: the usual causes](06-troubleshooting.md)** · 6 min
Faces drifting, dead motion, a clip that stutters at the cuts, credits gone with
nothing to show. Each with the actual fix.

**[Publishing a vertical clip](07-publishing.md)** · 5 min
Where a 9:16 clip goes, what each platform does to your file, and what to do
about the music.

## Shot devices

**[Shot devices: how one scene is filmed](08-shots.md)** · 7 min · pack `basic_shots`
The third layer after styles and story frames. Why a device here is the difference
between the first and last frame rather than a camera path. The six shots every clip
is built from.

**[Light: five sources and what each one costs](09-light.md)** · 6 min · pack `light_basics`
The decision that changes a frame more than shot size does. And why night scenes come
out as grey soup until you name one source.

**[Motion: what the engine will not ruin](10-motion.md)** · 6 min · pack `motion_basics`
"Nothing moves" and "everything smeared" are the same mistake. Five scenes where the
engine gets exactly one job.

**[Editing: half the clip lives at the cut](11-cuts.md)** · 6 min · pack `cut_basics`
Cut on the movement, colour bridge, jump of scale and the still frame. Devices that
cannot be shown on a single scene.

## Production mode

**[Series: a hero who comes back](12-series.md)** · 7 min · pack `series_kit`
Model sheet, library, episode naming and closing the loop. How to make episode two
look like episode one on purpose.

**[UGC: advertising that does not look like advertising](13-ugc.md)** · 6 min · pack `ugc_kit`
Why a beautiful frame hurts in UGC and what has to land in the first second and a
half.

**[Product shots: the five frames of a listing](14-product.md)** · 6 min · pack `product_kit`
Hero shot, hand, macro, environment, line-up — in the order a buyer asks the
questions silently.

## Teardowns

**[Teardown: why that clip travelled](15-teardown.md)** · 8 min · pack `teardown_kit`
The frame-by-frame method: what to count, what to ignore and how to tell a device
from a coincidence. The same method our signature styles were built with.

---
